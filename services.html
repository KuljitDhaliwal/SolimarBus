About Our Fleet

At Solimar Bus, we take pride in offering a modern fleet of over 160 coaches, minibuses, and luxury chauffeur-driven vehicles based in Barcelona. Each vehicle is designed for comfort, safety, and reliability—equipped with air-conditioning, Wi-Fi, and spacious interiors to ensure a smooth travel experience. Our professional drivers are trained, multilingual, and dedicated to delivering punctual, friendly, and efficient service for every journey. Whether you’re traveling locally within Spain or internationally between Morocco and Spain, Solimar Bus guarantees premium quality and peace of mind on every trip.

Transfers

Need a comfortable ride from the airport, hotel, or port?

Solimar Bus offers reliable transfer services tailored to your schedule and destination. Our fleet includes coaches, minibuses, and luxury vehicles—perfect for individuals, families, or groups. Whether it’s a hotel pickup, dinner outing, or city transfer, our professional drivers ensure a punctual, safe, and seamless experience from start to finish.

Visits
