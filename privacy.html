Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 25, 2025

At Solimar Bus Service, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the data you share with us when you use our website, mobile app, or contact us for bookings and inquiries.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:
* Personal Details: Your name, phone number, and any other details you provide when making a booking or inquiry.
* Trip Information: Pickup and drop-off locations, travel dates, and route preferences.
* Device Information: IP address, browser type, and device details for improving website performance.
* Communication Data: Messages or inquiries sent via WhatsApp, email, or contact forms.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:
* Process and confirm your bus bookings.
* Communicate updates or changes to your trips.
* Respond to customer inquiries and provide support.
* Improve our services, website, and user experience.
* Ensure safety, security, and compliance with local transportation regulations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or trade your personal data.

Your information may be shared only with:
* Authorized partners or agents for booking confirmation.
* Legal authorities when required by law.
* Service providers who help us operate our website, payment systems, or communication channels.

4. Data Security

We use secure technologies and procedures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, please note that no online transmission is 100% secure, and we encourage users to take care when sharing information online.

5. Cookies and Tracking

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and analyze website traffic. You can choose to disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not function properly.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:
* Request access to your personal data.
* Ask for corrections or deletions of your information.
* Withdraw consent for certain uses of your data.
To exercise these rights, please contact us using the details below.
